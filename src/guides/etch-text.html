Make a Label

The usual order.

  1. Type the TextThe label, one line of etching per line. Lower case is drawn as capitals.
  2. Set the Letter HeightThe capitals' height, baseline to top, in inches. It starts at 1/2 in.
  3. Check the PreviewIt shows the lines as they will be etched, between the baseline and the top of the capitals.
  4. DownloadDXF for the fab file, SVG for CorelDRAW, Illustrator or laser software.

Good to Know

  • Never fill it. The letters are lines, not shapes. Give them a hairline outline and no fill.
  • The size is the letters' own. The file is as wide and tall as the lines themselves, with no margin, so it lands where you place it.
  • Told apart. The zero is slashed and the one has a flag, so O and 0, and I and 1, can't be mixed up on a part.
  • Not in the font? A character it doesn't have is named in red under the text and left out.
  • How long it takes. Under the buttons, the inches of etching are how far the tool travels with the beam or bit down.
  • In Rhino. The font comes with a script that draws labels straight into a model. See Alexandria Etch Font.
  • It saves. The text and sizes stay in this browser.
