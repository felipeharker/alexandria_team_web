The Screen

The package's parts on the left, the open page on the right.

  1. The Parts. Document, Details, Page, CMF and Export, in the order of work. Each shows its own controls underneath.
  2. The Tools. Select picks and moves what's on the page. Dimension and Callout draw one each.
  3. The Info Box. The page's name, quantity and scope of work, set under Page. It grows to fit a longer scope.
  4. A Drawing. Your own SVG, PDF or image, placed at a scale inside the page's open area.
  5. A Dimension. Reads the drawing's real size, in feet and inches. It follows the drawing when that moves or changes scale.
  6. A Callout. A dot on what it points at, one leg, and its text in capitals.

Make a Package

The usual order.

  1. Fill In the DetailsThe project, the client, the company name and the client's logo. Red on the page is still the template's words.
  2. Add Pages and DrawingsA design page per view. Add Drawing or Image, or drop the files on the page.
  3. Set the ScaleLargest That Fits to start, or a standard scale. A picture needs its Real Width first.
  4. Dimension and Call OutTwo points and a side for a dimension; a point and a place for a callout's text.
  5. List the CMFPaint, vinyl, materials and electrical, each with a code, where it's used and a swatch.
  6. Export PDFExport lists what's unfinished first. Then Save Package File, and keep both with the project.

Good to Know

  • Red means not yet. A detail still empty shows the template's words in red. It never prints red: it prints as a dash, and Export tells you first.
  • Click on the drawing's own points. A pink square shows where a click will snap: an end of a line, a corner or a middle. That's what makes a dimension exact.
  • Which way a dimension runs. Move above or below the two points for the level distance, left or right for the plumb one. Hold Shift to measure point to point, and Alt to place the line freely.
  • Stacked dimensions. A dimension's line sits in steps of 1/4 in, so two of them land 1/2 in apart, as the standard asks.
  • The same scope on every page. Set it on one page and press Use on Every Page.
  • Futura on the title page. Under Export, Use Futura from This Computer reads Futura Bk BT from your computer, in Chrome or Edge. Until then the company name is in a close stand-in.
  • Undo. Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y. Delete removes what's selected, and the arrow keys nudge a drawing.
  • It saves, in this browser. For another computer or another person, Save Package File and open it there.
  • Not for cutting. The fab team cuts from the Fabrication Manager's files in Rhino. Linework from here is for bringing artwork into CAD at its true size.
