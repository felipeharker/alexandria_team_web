What each one does.

Sharing a Sheet

A CSV file is the sheet: export it, keep it with the project, and anyone can import it.

Import Reads Most Spreadsheets as They Are

  • Headers: Item or Description, UOM, Price or Rate, Qty and the like, in any order. Title rows above the header are skipped.
  • Units: sq ft, cu yd, linear feet, tons, each and other spellings become the codes above. One it doesn't know, like bag, stays as written.
  • Numbers: $1,234.50 reads fine, and so do European files (1.234,50, semicolons) and tab-separated text.
  • Gaps: a missing price or quantity becomes 0, a missing unit EA.

Good to Know

  • The sheet lives in this browser. It saves as you type, but another browser or computer starts empty, and clearing the browser's data clears it. Export a CSV to keep it.
  • Nothing leaves your computer. The Estimator has no server: CSV files are the only way a sheet goes anywhere.
  • Two tabs stay in step. An edit in one shows up in the other.
  • Exports are safe in Excel. A cell that starts with =, +, - or @ gets a leading ' so it stays text; import takes it off again.
