An image in, a grid whose cells are sized by its brightness out, like a halftone: an SVG at full size.

Open the Image Grid
  1. File Name

    Names the download.

    • It follows the image’s name until you type one of your own.
    • Your settings and the image are kept in this browser.
  2. Simple · Standard

    Simple holds the rest at standard values; Standard adds more.

    ImageRead: Brightness, Red, Green, Blue or Opacity. Sample: Average or Centre.
    RegionMatch the Image’s Proportions. Unticked, Fill or Stretch.
    GridEdge Cells: Whole or Clipped. Shape: The Cell or A Circle.
    ToneBlack, White, Midtones, and Sizes (2 to 32, empty is Any).
    Random CullStart, Cull, Seed.
    Back to StandardYour own settings return.
  3. Choose Image

    PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF or SVG. Nothing is uploaded.

    • Or drop a file anywhere, or paste one with Ctrl V.
    • It opens on a sample, or your last image. Use the Sample brings it back.
    • See-through parts read as white.
  4. Region

    The size of the area the grid fills.

    • Height follows the image. Standard can unlock it.
    • Units: Inches, Feet, Millimeters, Centimeters or Meters.
  5. Grid · Cell Size

    Hex, Square or Triangle, and the length of a cell’s side.

    • Smaller cells show more of the image.
  6. Scale

    Cell size from Min to Max, by the image under each cell.

    DefaultMax where the image is darkest, Min where it is lightest
    Largest Where the Image Is LightSwaps them
    Min · MaxA share of the full cell, 0 to 1. Min never passes Max.
    Scale 0The cell is left out
  7. Download SVG · Copy SVG

    The file, or its code on the clipboard.

    OutputReal size: feet come out as inches, meters as centimeters. Groups Region and Cells, no fills, hairline black.
    ShapesOne polygon per cell, or a true circle with A Circle. The image is not included.
    Sizes setA table lists each size’s side (a circle’s diameter) and its cell count, a punch or drill list. The SVG groups cells as Size-1, Size-2…
    The stats lineCells and region size, plus any culled or left out
    Over 30,000 cellsNothing is drawn. Use larger cells or a smaller region.
  8. Image · Filled

    Preview only. Neither changes the SVG.

    • Image: the picture, faint, under the cells.
    • Filled: cells solid, or as outlines.
  9. The Preview

    The grid as it will be drawn.

    • The readout gives the image’s value under the pointer, then X and Y from the bottom left corner.

Make an Image Grid

  1. Choose, drop or paste an image
  2. Set the region’s width and units
  3. Pick the grid shape and cell size
  4. Set Min and Max, and swap them for a light subject
  5. Download the SVG, then open it in Rhino or CorelDRAW
