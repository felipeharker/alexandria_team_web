The Screen

This is Simple.

  1. Simple, Standard. Simple shows the essentials. Standard shows every setting.
  2. Image. Choose Image, or drop or paste one. Nothing is uploaded.
  3. Region. The panel's width and units. The height follows the image.
  4. Grid, Cell Size. Square, Hex or Triangle, and a side's length.
  5. Scale. Max where the image is darkest, Min where it's lightest. Largest Where the Image Is Light swaps them.
  6. Download SVG. Or Copy SVG for its code.
  7. Image, Filled. Preview only: the image under the cells, and the cells filled or outlined.
  8. The region. Every cell, as the SVG will have them.

In Standard

Everything in Simple, and these.

  • Read. What sizes the cells: Brightness, Red, Green, Blue or Opacity.
  • Sample. Average reads the whole cell; Centre reads one point.
  • Match the Image's Proportions. Untick it to set the height, then Fill or Stretch.
  • Edge Cells. Whole or Clipped.
  • Shape. The Cell, or A Circle for round holes.
  • Tone. Black and White set what reads as fully dark and light. Midtones shifts the greys between.
  • Sizes. Limits the cells to a set number of sizes, for the punches or drills you have.
  • Random Cull. Takes out a share of the smallest cells at random.

Make a Grid

  1. Open the ImageHigh contrast reads best.
  2. Set the RegionThe panel's width.
  3. Choose the GridSmaller cells show more of the image.
  4. Set the ScaleMin, Max, and which way round.
  5. Fine-Tune, If WantedIn Standard.
  6. Download SVGThen import it into Rhino or CorelDRAW.

Good to Know

  • Full size. The SVG imports at its real size. Feet come out as inches, meters as centimeters.
  • Dark makes large. For holes lit from behind, tick Largest Where the Image Is Light.
  • One group per size. With Sizes set, each size is its own group in the SVG.
  • Clipped edges. A cut side doubles the boundary line: delete one before cutting.
  • Settings stay in this browser, the image too.
