Office Tools · Runs in Your Browser · Beta

The office's stock materials in one list: what each one is, its thickness, finish and size, how it's priced and who supplies it. Any of them goes onto a Material Estimator sheet in one click, already priced.

Open the Material Library →

A Material

One row per material, grouped by category.

  1. Category. Each one is a table of its own, and a button above to show only it.
  2. The Material. What it is, its thickness, finish and stock size.
  3. Price. Per pricing unit: EA for a sheet, SF, LF and the Estimator's other units.
  4. Add to Estimator. Opens the Material Estimator with the material on a new row, priced. Enter the quantity.

With the Estimator

Two ways in.

  1. From the LibraryAdd to Estimator on any material.
  2. On the SheetStart typing in a row's Material field and pick from the list: its category, unit, price and notes fill in.

The Columns

The library is a CSV file with these columns; the CSV template has three examples.

MaterialWhat it is, such as Cast Acrylic Sheet. The only column needed.
CategoryGroups the library: Metals, Plastics, Vinyl.
Thickness, Finish, SizeWith their units: 0.25 in, Clear, 4 ft x 8 ft.
Unit, Unit PriceHow it's priced, in the Estimator's units, and the dollars for one.
Supplier, NotesWho it comes from, and anything else.

Good to Know

  • Beta. The library is still being filled in.
  • Prices are a guide. Check them with the supplier before a quote.
  • On the sheet. A library material is named by its material, thickness and finish; its size, supplier and notes go in the row's Notes.
  • A material to add? Fill a row of the CSV template and send it in.
