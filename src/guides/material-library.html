The office's stock materials, with sizes and prices, ready to add to an Estimator sheet.

Open the Material Library →

A Material

One row per material, grouped by category.

  1. Category. A table each, and a button to show only it.
  2. The Material. Its thickness, finish and stock size.
  3. Price. Per unit: EA, SF, LF and the Estimator's others.
  4. Add to Estimator. Adds it to a sheet, priced. Enter the quantity.

With the Estimator

Two ways in.

  1. From the LibraryAdd to Estimator on any material.
  2. On the SheetType in a row's Material field and pick from the list.

Good to Know
