A Formula

Each formula is one line; open it for its recipe.

  1. Swatch. How the color looks on screen. A striped one means no source lists the color.
  2. Code and Name. The color the formula mixes, in its own system.
  3. MP Number. Matthews Paint's number for the formula, its product line and version.
  4. Colorants. What goes in, in grams per gallon, largest first.
  5. Find Matches. Opens the Color Matcher with this color in it.

Find a Formula

The usual order.

  1. Pick a SystemSherwin-Williams, Pantone, Matthews Paint, Benjamin Moore or Behr; or All.
  2. SearchBy code, name or MP number: 186, Iron Ore or 74163.
  3. Open ItIts colorants, with a total, and where it came from.
  4. Find MatchesIts nearest Sherwin-Williams, Pantone and 3M colors.

Reading a Formula

The sheets are Matthews Paint's; the library shows what matters for mixing.

MP NumberMatthews Paint's number for the formula. Quote it when ordering.
Product LineThe paint the formula is for: SV Satin for most, some SV Matte or N Satin. A color can have one formula per line.
VersionMatthews' revision of the formula.
Grams per GallonEach colorant's weight for a gallon. For a quart divide by 4, for a pint by 8.

Where the Swatches Come From

Every swatch is an on-screen approximation; the sheet itself has none. Each formula's fine print names its source.

SystemSwatch From
Sherwin-WilliamsSherwin-Williams' published colors, through PaintDB
PantoneThe PANTONE+ guides' Lab values; a few newer colors from third-party listings
Benjamin Moore, BehrTheir published colors, through PaintDB
Matthews PaintA third-party listing (hextoral.com). Matthews publishes no screen colors, so treat these as rough.

Good to Know

  • No client names. Formulas are filed by color, as Company_Code_Name.pdf, never by job.
  • Check a drawdown. A formula mixes what Matthews matched; check a sample against the brand color before a full batch.
  • Adding a formula. Name the sheet the same way, put it in the paint-formulas folder of the office tools repo, and rebuild the library (tools/build_colors.py).
  • From a color to its formula. In the Color Matcher, a match marked Formula on File links straight here.
