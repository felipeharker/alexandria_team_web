The office's Matthews Paint formulas, searchable: a color code in, its colorants and grams per gallon out.

Open the Color Library
  1. Search Code, Name or MP Number

    Narrows the list as you type.

    • Also matches the color system, such as Pantone.
  2. The Color Systems

    Shows one color system's formulas, or all of them.

    • The count at right is the formulas shown out of all on file.
  3. A Formula

    One line per formula. Click to open or close it.

    • The swatch is approximate, and striped when no color is on file.
    • The small line is the color system and its hex.
  4. MP Number

    The Matthews Paint formula number, with its product line and version.

    • A color has a formula for each line: 186 C has SV Matte and SV Satin, as two lines.
  5. Colorant · Name · Grams per Gallon

    What goes into a gallon of paint, with the total.

  6. Find Matches →

    Opens the Color Matcher with this color added.

    • Missing when the formula has no swatch.
  7. Printed

    The date the formula was printed and its PDF's file name.

Find a Formula

  1. Pick a color system, or leave All
  2. Search by code, name or MP number
  3. Open the formula's line
  4. Read the colorants and grams per gallon
  5. Find Matches to see its nearest colors
