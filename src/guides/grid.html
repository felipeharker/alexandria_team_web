A region and some attractors in, a hex, square or triangle grid whose cells scale toward them out: an SVG at full size.

Open the Parametric Grid
  1. File Name

    Names the download.

    • It starts as Grid. Everything you set is kept in this browser.
  2. Region

    The size of the area the grid fills.

    • Units: Inches, Feet, Millimeters, Centimeters or Meters.
    • Resizing keeps each attractor at the same place on the region.
  3. Grid · Cell Size · Edge Cells

    The cell shape, its side length, and the border.

    Cell SizeA side’s length, in the region’s units
    WholeOnly the cells that fit
    ClippedCells cut at the border. The cut sides double the outline.
  4. Scale

    Min at the nearest attractor, growing to Max at Reach.

    Min · MaxA share of the full cell, 0 to 1. Min never passes Max.
    Scale 0The cell is left out
    Largest at the AttractorSwaps them: Max at the attractor
    ReachDistance where the far size is reached. Empty is Auto, the farthest cell.
  5. Random Cull

    Takes out a share of the cells at random.

    Start0 to 1. The share of cells nearest the attractors that are kept.
    Cull0 to 1. The share of the rest taken out.
    SeedWhich ones. A new number picks a new set.
  6. Attractors

    The points and curves the cell sizes follow. Up to 50.

    Point 1, Curve 1…Click one to select it. × removes it.
    X · YPlace the selected point, from the region’s bottom left corner.
    Straight · SmoothA selected curve: lines between its points, or a curve through them.
    Add Point · Add CurveThen click the preview. Press again, or Esc, to cancel.
    No attractorsEvery cell at Max
  7. Download SVG · Copy SVG

    The file, or its code on the clipboard.

    Include the Attractors in the SVGAdds an Attractors group: a small circle per point, a line per curve
    OutputReal size: feet come out as inches, meters as centimeters. Groups Region and Cells, no fills, hairline black.
    The stats lineCells, region size, reach, and how many were culled
    Over 30,000 cellsNothing is drawn. Use larger cells or a smaller region.
  8. The Preview

    Place and move attractors here. The readout gives X and Y.

    Click or drag a handleMoves it. Clicking another attractor selects it.
    Click empty spaceMoves the selected point, or adds a point to the end of the selected curve
    Click on the selected curveInserts a point there
    Double-click a handleRemoves that point
    Delete BackspaceRemoves the selected point
    EscCancels Add Point or Add Curve
    Undo, zoom, panNone

Make a Grid

  1. Set the region’s size and units
  2. Choose the grid shape and cell size
  3. Place the attractors on the preview
  4. Set Min and Max
  5. Cull some cells, if wanted
  6. Download the SVG, then open it in Rhino or CorelDRAW
