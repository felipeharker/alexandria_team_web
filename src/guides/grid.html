The Screen

Controls on the left, the preview on the right.

  1. Region. The panel's width, height and units. The SVG comes out at this size.
  2. Grid. Square, Hex or Triangle cells.
  3. Cell Size, Edge Cells. Cell Size is the length of a side. Whole keeps the cells that fit; Clipped fills the region and cuts the edge cells at its boundary.
  4. Scale. Each cell is scaled about its centre, from Min at the attractor to Max at the Reach distance and beyond. Empty Reach means the farthest cell. Largest at the Attractor swaps them.
  5. Attractor. A Point, or a Curve drawn Straight or Smooth. A point's X and Y can be typed, from the region's bottom left corner.
  6. Download SVG. Saves the linework, named after the file name at the top. Copy SVG copies its code instead.
  7. The attractor. Click or drag to place the point. For a curve, click to add points, click the curve to insert one, drag to move, double-click to remove.
  8. The region. Its boundary and every cell, as the SVG will have them. The pointer's X and Y show at the top right.

Make a Grid

The usual order. The preview updates as you go.

  1. Set the RegionThe panel's size, in the units it will be made in.
  2. Choose the GridSquare, Hex or Triangle, the cell size, and Whole or Clipped.
  3. Place the AttractorClick the preview for a point, or switch to Curve and click out its points.
  4. Set the ScaleMin and Max for the smallest and largest cells, and Reach for how far the change spreads.
  5. Cull, If WantedRandom Cull takes a share of the cells out at random from a Start distance on, like Grasshopper's Random Reduce.
  6. Download SVGInto the project's folder, then import it into Rhino, CorelDRAW or Illustrator.

Good to Know

  • Full size. The SVG is in the region's units, so it imports at its real size. Feet come out as inches and meters as centimeters, since SVG has neither.
  • Clean linework. One closed shape per cell and the boundary, with no fills, in groups named Region and Cells. Tick Include the Attractor in the SVG to add it too.
  • Random Cull. Of the cells at least Start from the attractor (empty means all of them), Cull percent are taken out at random. The same Seed always takes out the same cells, and they stay put while you move the attractor; change it for another pick.
  • Clipped edges. A clipped cell's cut side lies on the boundary, so that line is there twice: delete one before cutting.
  • Settings stay. Everything, the attractor too, is remembered in this browser.
