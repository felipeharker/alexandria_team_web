Open Photo Elevation
  1. Straighten · Crop · Dimension

    The three steps, in order.

    • Crop frames the straightened photo: around your four lines, until you drag the frame's edges.
    • Whole Photo and Around the Lines set the frame back.
  2. Choose Photo

    A screenshot, a street view image or a survey photo: chosen, dropped or pasted.

    • It stays in your browser, with your work on it.
    • The tool opens on a sample, a storefront 44'-0" wide and 20'-0" tall with a 7'-0" door.
  3. Level Lines

    The two blue lines: drag each end onto something level on the building, such as the roofline, a sill or the ground line.

    • The end you drag is shown enlarged beside it.
    • One high and one low straightens best.
  4. Plumb Lines

    The two orange lines: drag each end onto something plumb, such as a corner, a door's side or a column.

    • One far left and one far right straightens best.
    • Reset Lines puts all four back.
  5. Fit

    Shows the whole photo again, after scrolling to zoom or dragging to pan.

Straighten the Photo

  1. Open the photo
  2. Put the blue lines on two level edges
  3. Put the orange lines on two plumb edges
  4. Open Crop and tighten the frame, if it needs it
  1. The Sheet

    Click two points on the photo, then click where the dimension goes.

    • The second point locks level or plumb with the first, whichever the pointer is nearer.
    • Its line sits 1/2" from the points or further, in steps of 1/4". Alt places it anywhere.
    • Click a dimension to select it, then drag its line, or an end, which stays level or plumb with the other. Delete removes it.
  2. Dimensions

    Every dimension and its length; Set marks one whose real length was typed.

  3. Label

    Text on the line above the value, in capitals, such as HEIGHT OF BUILDING.

  4. Real Length

    The selected dimension's true size, such as 24'-6": every other dimension is scaled from it.

    • A number alone is inches.
    • The first dimension you draw asks for it.
    • On a photo taken at an angle, set one width and one height. Each way then takes its own scale, and the panel says when it wants both.
  5. Sheet

    Tabloid, the design package's page, or Letter, turned to suit the photo.

  6. Download PDF · PNG

    The sheet as shown.

    • PDF: the photo as a picture, the dimensions as lines and text.
    • PNG: 200 dpi.

Dimension It

  1. Click two points, then where the dimension goes
  2. Type its real length
  3. Add the others, and label them
  4. Download the sheet
