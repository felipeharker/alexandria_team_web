Good to Know

  • Screens aren't paint. Check a match against a physical chip or a drawdown before ordering.
  • 3M's colors are shown unlit. A lit film looks brighter: go by 3M's matching Pantone.
  • The list lives in this browser. Export a CSV to keep it.
