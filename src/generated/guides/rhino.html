From model to package

The usual order. Skip any step a job doesn't need.

  1. Create ProjectName it, pick a folder. Your model moves into models.
  2. Populate ModelLayers, styles, the render backdrop and light.
  3. Model the signFull size in inches, at the origin, facing −Y, on DESIGN sublayers.
  4. Materials & lightSet Material on DESIGN, Set Light on lit faces.
  5. RenderRight click Day, then Night: saves day_#.jpg and night_#.jpg.
  6. Standard drawingsDetails and blocks from the libraries.
  7. Draw & dimensionElevations flat in Top view, Standard Dimension.
  8. Lay out pagesCreate Document, pages, drawings, details, renders.
  9. Export PDFDESIGN_<project>_v#.pdf in design_package.

The buttons

What each one does, in a line or two.

Alexandria Setup AlexandriaSetup

Click
Adds the Alexandria Tools tab and installs the DM display modes. Run once after installing.
Right
Set Panels: arranges the right-hand panels to the standard.

Create Project

Click
Name the project and choose a folder. Copies the project template there and saves your model as <project>_model_v1.3dm.

Never writes over an existing folder.

Populate Model

Click
Inches and tolerances, the DESIGN and DETAILS layers, the annotation styles, the render backdrop and the world light.

Safe to click again: it only adds what's missing.

Set Material

Click
Pick a parent layer. Each sublayer gets a plaster material in its layer colour: MAT_1, MAT_2…

Recolour a layer, click again, and its material follows.

Set Light

Click
Select faces or solids, then choose a light type (below). They become one shared V-Ray light on DESIGN::ILLUMS::<type>.

Day and Night switch these lights for you.

Apply Day Settings

Click
Light blue sky, neutral light, sign lights down to daylight, 4K output.
Right
Render Day and Save: asks Save with sky (a JPEG with it, or a transparent PNG), then renders the active view to the renders folder as day_#.

Apply Night Settings

Click
Navy to mauve sky, dim light, sign lights back on.
Right
Render Night and Save: the same, as night_#.jpg.

Neither button hides layers: show or hide the wall, ground and figure yourself.

Populate Detail

Click
Pick a detail from the library (DTL-01 to DTL-13) and click where it goes. It arrives grouped, in the standard layers and styles.

Fill in its blanks (paint, LED colour) once, in the model.

Populate Block

Click
Pick a north arrow, section indicator, dot callout, graphic scale, person, car or tree, and click where it goes. Symbols come in at paper size on a page and at the annotation scale in a model, numbered for you; the rest at true size.

Change a callout's number by editing its text.

Standard Dimension

Click
A dimension in the Alexandria style, on DETAILS::ANNOTATIONS::DIMENSIONS.
Right
Standard Leader: a callout, on …::CALLOUTS.

Layout Manager

Click
One window for the whole design package: pages, project and client details, drawings, details, colors and materials, renders, the PDF and the project facts. See its parts below.

Save as Rhino 7 · Save as Rhino 8

Click
Writes a copy next to the model as <name>_r7.3dm or <name>_r8.3dm. You keep working in the original.

Where the sign goes

Populate Model's backdrop expects the sign at the origin, its face on the wall's plane, facing −Y.

Set Light's four types

Section through a letter, wall on the left. Yellow is what glows.

Front lit channelFace lights forward · 4, white
Reverse lit channelHalo on the wall behind · 4, white
Push through bodyThe whole solid glows · 2, white
Push through faceA dark face, faintly lit · 0.5, charcoal

Layers you'll see

DESIGN::<part>The sign's parts. Set Material colours them.
DESIGN::ILLUMS::<type>Lit faces from Set Light.
DETAILS::2D2D detail linework: CUT, VISIBLE, FILL-…
DETAILS::ANNOTATIONSDIMENSIONS and CALLOUTS.
RENDER::…Wall, ground, scale figure and the world light. Off in every placed view.
LAYOUT::…The pages' parts. VARIABLE is the red per-project text.
