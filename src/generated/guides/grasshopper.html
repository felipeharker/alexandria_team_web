The Basic Chain

The template along the top of the master definition: a 144 × 96 in region becomes square cells, each cell gets a value from its distance to a curve, the values scale the cells, a share of them is culled, and the rest are previewed.

Swap Any One for Another

Every grid and Voronoi component outputs Cells; every remap reads Geometry and outputs Values. Change a step of the chain by swapping its component and keeping the wires.

Two Runs to Know

Good to Know

  • Nothing bakes by itself. Only Bake to Layer and the parameter components touch the document, and only when you press their button input.
  • Lists stay parallel. An item that fails gives a null in its place, so values still line up with their cells.
  • Hover a port for its description and default, written from the component's own spec.
  • Feet Inches turns lengths into labels like 2'-6 1/2"; Layer Geometry keeps a Rhino layer live on the canvas.
