**To get it:** restart Rhino twice, then click **Alexandria Setup**.

### Claude Connectors

A connector lets Claude work in a program on your PC: the open Rhino model, or the open CorelDRAW document. **Alexandria Setup** now installs both, with nothing to do by hand.

- **A window opens the first time** and shows what it installs.
- **Rhino** needs 8.19 or later.
- **CorelDRAW:** open your document first. Claude never starts CorelDRAW by itself.
- **Afterwards,** quit Claude Desktop from its tray icon and open it again.

[Claude Connectors](/docs/install/#connectors-for-claude-and-grok) has the details.

### Without Rhino

Get **AI connectors** from [All Downloads](/wiki/downloads/#ai-connectors), unzip it and double-click **Install AI Connectors**.
