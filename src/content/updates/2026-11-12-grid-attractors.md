title: Several Attractors on the Parametric Grid
date: 2026-11-12T17:30

The [Parametric Grid](/grid/) takes as many attractors as a panel needs, points and curves in any mix. Reload its page to get the new version.

- **Add Point** and **Add Curve** make your next click on the preview start a new one. Each curve is drawn **Straight** or **Smooth** on its own.
- **Each cell scales toward the attractor nearest to it.** Two points make two pools of small cells; a curve with a point beside it pulls the cells toward whichever is closer. Reach and Random Cull measure to the nearest attractor too.
- **The list under Attractors** shows them all. Click one, there or on the preview, to select it: a click on the preview then moves that point or adds to that curve. **×** removes one, and so does double-clicking it on the preview.
- **Include the Attractors in the SVG** puts every one of them in the file, in a group named Attractors.

A grid you saved before opens with the attractor it had. See the [quick guide](/grid/guide/).
