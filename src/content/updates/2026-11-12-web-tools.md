Two new web tools, a north arrow, scale bar and colors on the Site Plan, and the Material Estimator now picks from the office's own materials. Reload a tool's page to get the new version.

### Material Library <span class="ax-badge">Beta</span>

A new Fabrication tool at [/materials](/materials/): the office's stock materials in one list, grouped by category.

- Each material has its thickness, finish and stock size, how it's priced, its price and its supplier, so an estimate starts from the office's numbers rather than a search.
- **Add to Estimator** opens the Material Estimator with that material on a new line, its category, unit and price filled in. Enter the quantity and the line is done.
- It's in beta because it's still being filled in. Materials go in as rows of a CSV file; the [CSV template](/templates/material-library/) shows the columns, with three examples.

See its [quick guide](/materials/guide/).

### Braille Lettering Designer

A new Design tool at [/braille](/braille/): type a sign's text and download its braille, ready to place below the raised lettering.

- **Grade 2 braille, as ADA signs need**, from liblouis, the open-source translator that screen readers and braille software use. Contractions such as *st*, *en* and *con* become single cells, and capitals and numbers get their signs. **Grade 1** spells every word out instead.
- **The SVG is the real size**: one circle per raised dot, in inches, so it imports into Rhino, CorelDRAW or Illustrator at full size.
- **ADA dimensions to start with**: dots 0.06 in across, 0.1 in apart in a cell, cells 0.25 in apart and lines 0.4 in apart, the middle of each range in the 2010 ADA Standards. Others can be typed, and any outside the ADA's ranges is flagged.
- The preview shows each cell's six places, and the braille is also given as braille characters, to paste into a translator and read back when proofing.

Type the text as it should read, *Restroom* rather than *RESTROOM*: braille capitalizes only the start of a sentence, names and acronyms. See its [quick guide](/braille/guide/).

### Site Plan

- **North arrow and scale bar.** Both sit bottom left on the plan, on a white plate, and come out on the exported PNG. The scale bar is a round length in feet (miles past a mile), measured on the map at its current zoom. Untick **North Arrow** or **Scale Bar** to leave either off.
- **A color per sign.** **Circle Color** sets the selected sign's circle and label to any color, picked or typed as a hex code. Red stays the standard for every new plan, and **Reset to Red** goes back to it.

### Material Estimator

- Typing in a line's **Material** field lists the Material Library's materials. Picking one fills in its category, pricing unit, unit price and notes (its size and supplier).
- **Material Library** in the top bar opens the library.
