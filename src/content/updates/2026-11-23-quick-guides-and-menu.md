### Quick Guides

Every web tool's guide is now a drawing of its screen with numbered dots. Point at a dot for the control's name, click it for what it does, and follow the few steps under the drawing. Each guide was checked against its tool. They are all on one page: [Quick Guides](/guides/).

### The Menu

The menu on the left lists sections, and each opens a page with what it holds: a tool category shows its tools, [Suppliers](/wiki/suppliers/) the suppliers, [Templates](/templates/) the templates, [Downloads](/wiki/downloads/) every file.
