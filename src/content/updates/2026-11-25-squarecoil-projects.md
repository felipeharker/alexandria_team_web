title: Claude Reads Whole SquareCoil Projects
date: 2026-11-25T16:30

version: '2.21'
---

**To get it:** restart Rhino twice, click **Install Connectors**, tick **SquareCoil** and install. Then quit Claude Desktop from its tray icon and open it again.

Claude could read the design request lists. Now it reads any project.

- **Find a project** by number, name, site address, city, customer, status, salesperson or project manager. Try "what do we have at 2026 W Gray?"
- **Ask anything about it:** the client and contacts, who it bills to, the scope of work, the notes on each milestone, the design requests and their revisions, the tasks.
- **Its files:** Claude lists a project's documents and can save one to your Downloads folder, in `SquareCoil`.
- **Customers:** look one up by name or address.

Still read-only, and still your own login. SquareCoil's activity log shows the project tabs Claude opened under your name, the same as when you open them yourself.

[Claude Connectors](/docs/install/#connectors-for-claude-and-grok) has the details.
