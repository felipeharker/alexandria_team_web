**To get it:** restart Rhino twice, then click **Install Connectors**.

Claude and Grok get the SquareCoil, Rhino/Grasshopper and CorelDRAW connectors from the team's plugins, so **Alexandria Setup** no longer adds them to Claude's own settings. It still installs what they run on your PC, and your SquareCoil login.

- **Each connector twice in Claude?** That's the old entry beside the plugin. **Install Connectors** takes the old ones out, then quit Claude Desktop from its tray icon and open it again.
- **New PC?** **Alexandria Setup** once, as before. Grok users add the three from [AI Connectors](/docs/connectors/#grok).

[AI Connectors](/docs/connectors/) has the rest.
