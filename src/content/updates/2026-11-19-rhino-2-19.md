**To get it:** restart Rhino twice, then click **Alexandria Setup**.

### The Alexandria Template

A new model can start with the standards already in it. Choose **File › New › Alexandria**, then click **Create Project** as usual.

- **Set up like Populate Model:** units, layers, annotation styles, the wall, ground, scale figure and world light.
- **Ready to render.** Click **Day Render** or **Night Render** once for the sky.
- **The design package's first pages are there,** waiting for the project's details in the Layout Manager.

Your own default template stays as it was, and **Populate Model** still sets up any other model.

### Also in 2.19

- **Populate Model.** The wall and ground layers stay locked after a save.
