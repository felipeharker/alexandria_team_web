The Rhino and Grasshopper packages in the office's shared folder are now 2.19. With automatic updates on, Rhino installs them the next time it starts; restart once more to load them, then click **Alexandria Setup**, which adds the template. [Install and Update](/docs/install/#updating) has the details.

### The Alexandria Template

A new model can start with everything in it. In Rhino, choose **File › New** and pick **Alexandria**, then click **Create Project** as usual.

- **Set up as Populate Model leaves a model.** Inches and the standard tolerances, every standard layer with its colour and print width, the eight Alexandria annotation styles, and the wall, ground, scale figure and world light.
- **Ready to render.** V-Ray is the renderer, with the standard camera, light and 4K image size. Click **Day Render** or **Night Render** once for the sky.
- **The design package has its first pages.** `TITLE`, `PROJECT INFO`, `PAGE 1` and `COLORS, MATERIALS & FINISHES` are there, waiting for the project's details in the Layout Manager.
- **Tidy.** `DESIGN` is the current layer and Alexandria Dimension the current style, Rhino's own spare layers and styles are gone, and the Perspective view opens in DM-WORKING on the scale figure at the wall.

Nothing in it is drawn by hand: it's made by the tools themselves, so it follows the standards. Your own default template stays as it was, and the buttons work the same in any model: **Populate Model** still sets up one that didn't start from the template.

The file is on the office drive too, in `alexandria_packages`, as `alexandria_template.3dm`.
