### Vectorizer

A new Design tool at [/vectorizer](/vectorizer/): a PNG or JPG, such as a client's low-resolution logo, becomes an SVG of clean vector shapes.

- **Open, drop or paste** the image. The Vectorizer finds its colors, leaving out the in-between shades that blurry edges and JPEG compression leave behind.
- **Choose the colors.** Ticked colors are kept, unticked ones merge into the nearest kept color, and **Omit** leaves one out, such as a white background. **Keep a Color** and **Omit a Color** pick one straight off the image.
- **Precision** runs from 1, fewer and smoother curves, to 10, a close fit that keeps small detail. Corners stay sharp and straight edges straight at every setting, and thin strokes keep their width.
- **Clean Geometry** makes nearly level and plumb edges exact and near-circles true circles, for artwork drawn with a ruler and compass.
- **The SVG** has one shape per color, and neighbouring colors share the same edge, with no gaps or overlaps, so each color can be cut as its own layer. The image never leaves your browser.

See its [quick guide](/vectorizer/guide/).

### Parametric Grid

A new Design tool at [/grid](/grid/): patterned panels as clean linework, without opening Grasshopper.

- **A square, hex or triangle grid** on a panel of the size you set, in inches, feet, millimeters, centimeters or meters.
- **Cells scale toward an attractor** you place on the preview: a point, or a curve of clicked points, straight or smooth. Each cell runs from **Min** at the attractor to **Max** at the **Reach** distance, or the other way round.
- **Whole** keeps the cells that fit inside the panel; **Clipped** fills it and cuts the edge cells at its boundary.
- **Random Cull** thins the cells away from the attractor, like Grasshopper's Random Reduce: **Start** leaves the nearest cells alone and **Cull** takes out a share of the rest, with a **Seed** to try another set.
- **Download SVG** saves the boundary and one closed shape per cell at full size, for Rhino, CorelDRAW or fabrication.

See its [quick guide](/grid/guide/).

### Site Plan

- **Several signs per plan.** **Add Sign** places another sign with the next click or search, each with its own label and radius. Click a sign in the list, or its label on the map, to select it; the Label and radius fields then edit that sign.
