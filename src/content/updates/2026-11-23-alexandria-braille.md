### Alexandria Braille

The office's own braille font. Set at 36 pt, with lines 28.8 pt apart, its dots are at ADA sign sizes, each a filled circle.

Translate the sign's text in the [Braille Lettering Designer](/braille/), copy the braille characters and paste them in the font. Typed letters come out uncontracted, so for a sign, paste.

[Its page](/wiki/downloads/braille-font/) has the font and the specimen. The font is also in the Alexandria folder on the office drive.
