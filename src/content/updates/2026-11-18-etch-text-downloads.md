A font for etching part labels, a web tool to type with it, and one page with every download.

### Etch Text <span class="ax-badge">Beta</span>

A new Fabrication tool at [/etch-text](/etch-text/): type a part label, such as `A1` or `P1-1`, and download it as single lines for a laser or router to follow once.

- **SVG or DXF, at real size**, in inches. The DXF is on the `ETCH` layer, like the Fabrication Manager's.
- **SVG as Text** keeps the words editable, where the font is installed.
- It's in beta because its DXF hasn't been run on the laser or the router yet.

See its [quick guide](/etch-text/guide/).

### Alexandria Etch

The office's own single-line font: one line down the middle of every letter, a slashed zero, and only straight lines and true arcs. [Its page](/wiki/downloads/etch-font/) has the font, a Rhino script that draws labels into a model, and the specimen. The font file is on the office drive too, in `alexandria_packages`.
