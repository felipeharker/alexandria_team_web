### Etch Text <span class="ax-badge">Beta</span>

A new Fabrication tool at [/etch-text](/etch-text/): type a part label, such as `A1`, and download it as single lines for a laser or router.

- **SVG or DXF, at real size.** The DXF is on the `ETCH` layer.
- **Beta:** its DXF hasn't been run on a machine yet.

See its [quick guide](/etch-text/guide/).

### Alexandria Etch

The office's own single-line font. [Its page](/wiki/downloads/etch-font/) has the font, a Rhino script and the specimen.
