A new section, **Parametric Tools**, for patterned panels. The [Parametric Grid](/grid/) moves there from Design Tools, beside a new tool, the [Image Grid](/image-grid/). It's in beta.

### Image Grid <span class="ax-badge">Beta</span>

Grasshopper's Image Sampler on a grid, without opening Grasshopper: each cell of a square, hex or triangle grid is sized by the image under it, so a photo, a logo or a gradient shows in the panel's cells, as a halftone does.

- **Open an image,** or drop or paste one. It stays in your browser. A sample, a soft letter A, is there to start.
- **Simple** shows the essentials: the region's width, the grid, the cell size, and Min and Max. The height follows the image.
- **Standard** shows everything: brightness or one color or opacity, tone, a set number of **Sizes** for the punches or drills on hand (listed with how many of each), circles in place of the cells, Clipped edges and a random cull.
- **Download SVG** gives full-size linework, as the Parametric Grid's does, with a group per size.

See its [quick guide](/image-grid/guide/).
