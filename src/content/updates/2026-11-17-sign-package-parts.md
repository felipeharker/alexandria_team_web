---
title: Change a Drawing's Colors and Lines in the Sign Package Maker
date: 2026-11-17T18:00
area: Web Tools
---

The pages of a sign package are the standard's and stay fixed. The design on them is yours, and the [Sign Package Maker](/sign-package/) now lets you change it as CorelDRAW or Illustrator would. Reload its page to get the new version. The tool is still in beta.

- **Open a drawing's parts.** Click a drawing twice, or press **Edit Its Parts**. Each shape in it can then be clicked. Shift-click, or drag a box, for several.
- **Fill and line.** A part takes a **Fill** and a **Line** of any color, or one of the package's CMF swatches, a **Line Weight** in points and an **Opacity**.
- **A whole color at once.** **Select Same Fill** takes every part with the selected color, so one change recolors them all.
- **Move, restack, delete.** Drag the selected parts or use the arrow keys, **Bring to Front** or **Send to Back**, and Delete.
- **Dimension a part.** **Add Their Dimensions** draws the width and height of the box around what's selected: a letter, a logo, a cabinet.
- **Your file isn't touched.** The changes belong to the drawing as it's placed. **Put It Back as Its File Has It** clears them, and the PDF and the 1:1 linework follow them.
- **Parallel dimensions.** With the Dimension tool, the bar now offers **Level or Plumb**, as before, and **Parallel**, which measures straight from one point to the other at any angle, for a sloped edge or a diagonal.

A drawing has to be all shapes to be opened this way: save an SVG or a PDF with its text as outlines (curves), and without pictures or gradients. See the [quick guide](/sign-package/guide/).
