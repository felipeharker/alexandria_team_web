The Wiki has a page for each supplier whose colors and materials go on the drawings, all laid out the same way: what it is, its product lines, how its codes read, how we call it out, and its chart.

- **[Matthews Paint](/wiki/suppliers/matthews-paint/):** the sign paint, with every color on the Matthews chart and its LRV.
- **[Sherwin-Williams](/wiki/suppliers/sherwin-williams/):** the numbers buildings are specified in, with each color's hex code.
- **[Pantone](/wiki/suppliers/pantone/):** the numbers brand colors arrive in, with each color's CMYK build.
- **[3M Films](/wiki/suppliers/3m/):** translucent and opaque vinyl, with the Pantone match 3M prints for each film.
- **[Eastern Metal Supply](/wiki/suppliers/eastern-metal-supply/):** aluminum sheet, coil, sign frames and raceways, with stock codes and sheet sizes.

Each long chart has a **Find** box, and each page ends with the supplier's own document to open. The suppliers' sites are on [Reference Links](/reference/links/) too.

In the sidebar, the tool categories now sit together under **Tools**, and CSV Templates is **Templates**.
