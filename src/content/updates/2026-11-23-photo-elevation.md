**Permitting Tools** has its first tool, and moves up to third on the home page and in the menu.

### Photo Elevation <span class="ax-badge">Beta</span>

A photo of a building in, a dimensioned elevation out, for the permit drawings.

- **Open a photo:** a screenshot, a street view image or a survey photo. It stays in your browser.
- **Straighten** it with four lines: two along level edges of the building, two up plumb ones.
- **Dimension** it: click two points, type one real length, and every other dimension is scaled from it. Add a label above any value, such as HEIGHT OF BUILDING.
- **Download PDF**, or a PNG. The dimensions are in the Alexandria standard.

See its [quick guide](/photo-elevation/guide/).
