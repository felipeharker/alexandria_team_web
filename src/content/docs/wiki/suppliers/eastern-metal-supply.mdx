### Mounting

- **EZ Clip,** a strip that mates with itself, to hang a cabinet or a panel and lift it off again: `40-63-008` with 3/8" of lift, `40-63-007` with 5/8", both 144" long. The 3/8" clip also comes cut and drilled at 2", 7-5/8" and 13-1/8".
- **H-retainer** `24-63-160`, 1-3/4" by 16' 6", holds 1/4" sign faces.

## Reading a Code

- **Three parts, as in `24-63-230`.** The first is the product family: `50` and `51` are sheet, `49` is coil, `24` is sign extrusions, `10` is flat bar. The middle is usually the alloy, `61` for 6061 and `63` for 6063, or a finish, such as `WH` for white, `MB` for bronze and `CA` for clear anodized.
- **Sheet sizes read thickness x width x length,** in inches: `.063 x 48 x 96` is a 4' x 8' sheet 1/16" thick. Coil reads thickness x width x length of the coil.
- **Letters on the end** add to it: `PVC` is a protective film on one side, `W` is white.
- **Alloy and temper,** as in `5052-H32`: the alloy is the metal's recipe and the temper how hard it has been worked. 5052 bends well and resists corrosion; 3003 is the softer general-purpose sheet; 6061-T6 is the structural one, and cracks if bent tight.
- **Mill order** means it isn't stocked: the mill makes it to order, with a minimum quantity and a wait. **Special order** is ordered in for the job.

## How We Use It

- **On a detail,** metal is called out by thickness and part, as in *.063" ALUMINUM SIDEWALLS*, *.125" ALUMINUM FACE* and *1/8" ALUMINUM BACKER*. The stock lists below show which sizes those thicknesses come in, so a face or a backer can be laid out on a sheet that exists.
- **Check the sheet before the size.** A backer wider than 60", or 72" in a few thicknesses, has a seam or needs plate. The list says which.
- **Channel letter returns** are bent from coil, .040" thick and 5.3" wide, already painted white, bronze, red or black on the outside and white inside.
- **Backs called out as painted white** can be cut from painted sheet, white on both sides, with no trip to the paint booth.
- **Weight for an install** starts from the list too: the pounds per sheet, scaled to the part's area.
- **Pricing** belongs in the [Material Library](/materials/), which lists a material's supplier, and the [Material Estimator](/estimator/). The catalog has no prices.

## Finishes and Notes

- **Mill finish** is bare aluminum, as rolled. It gets painted in [Matthews](/wiki/suppliers/matthews-paint/).
- **Painted sheet** names two colors, front then back: *Bronze/White* is bronze on one side and white on the other. It comes with a PVC film over the paint.
- **White isn't one white.** The Florida branch's white is Polar White; North Carolina's and Texas' is Bright White.
- **Anodized sheet** is 5005 with a clear, bronze or extra dark anodized coat and a PVC film on one side.
- **ACM** is two thin aluminum skins on a plastic core, flat and light, and it can be printed on directly. Three makes, by skin thickness: ValuMetal (.004"), EMS ACM (.006", matte) and PolyMetal (.008").
- The catalog pages are dated 2023 to 2026. Stock and codes change, so confirm with the EMS sales desk before a code goes on an order.
