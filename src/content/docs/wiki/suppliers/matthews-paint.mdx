## Product Lines

Matthews sells one paint system in a few versions. They take the same colors, so a color number is worth little without its line and gloss.

| Line | What It Is |
|---|---|
| MAP (Matthews Acrylic Polyurethane) | The original line. |
| Satin MAP, Satin VOC MAP | Satin versions, the second with less solvent. Codes on the chart beginning `SVOC` are this line. |
| MAP-LV | Low VOC, in gloss and satin. |
| MAP Ultra Low VOC | The lowest-solvent line, in gloss and satin. |
| Clears | Clear coats over the color, one of them graffiti-resistant, and Super Satin Clear. |
| Primers | Epoxy primers in gray, white and black, and a polyester primer surfacer. |

The office's own formulas, in the [Color Library](/colors/library/), say which line each one is mixed in: `SV Satin` for most, some `SV Matte` or `N Satin`.

## Reading a Code

- **`MP` and five digits** is a color formula: `MP24846` is Rally Red. Matthews gives a new MP number to every color it matches, so a brand color matched for one job has its own.
- **Digits ending in `SP`** are colors Matthews mixes at the factory and sells ready to spray: `42204SP` Gloss Black, `41342SP` Brushed Aluminum. The Proseco metallics are `48101SP` to `48108SP`.
- **LRV** is light reflectance value: how much light the color sends back, from 0 (black) to 100 (white). ADA signs need it: the letters and their background have to sit far apart in LRV to be read.

## How We Use It

- **On a detail,** a blank that reads *PAINTED ______* takes a Matthews color: the MP number, the name and the gloss, as in *PAINTED MP24846 RALLY RED, SATIN*. The [Typical Signage Types](/wiki/signage-types/) show where those blanks are.
- **For a brand color** given as Pantone or Sherwin-Williams, look in the [Color Library](/colors/library/) first. If the office has mixed it before, the formula is there with its MP number.
- **For a color nobody has matched,** the [Color Matcher](/colors/) finds the nearest colors and says when one of them has a Matthews formula on file. Otherwise the paint supplier matches it and sends back a new MP number.
- **A raceway** is painted to match the wall behind it, so that color usually comes from the building. See [Sherwin-Williams](/wiki/suppliers/sherwin-williams/).

## Finishes and Notes

- The chart shows five gloss levels: **High Gloss, Semi-Gloss, Satin Gloss, Matte** and **Textured**. Satin is the office's usual, as most formulas in the Color Library are.
- **Metallics** shift with the angle and the light, and a screen can't show that. Three of them, Brilliant Gold, Aztec Gold and Aztec Copper, are sprayed over a named base color.
- The chart stars a few codes without saying why. Ask the paint supplier before ordering one.
- Every swatch below is the chart's color as a screen shows it. Pick from a physical Matthews chart or a sprayed sample, never from this page.
