## Product Lines

The chart is Sherwin-Williams' ColorSnap list. Its numbers fall into a few runs.

| Numbers | What They Are |
|---|---|
| SW 0001 to SW 0080 | Historic colors. They have no place on the store display, so no locator number. |
| SW 6000 to SW 7757 | The main ColorSnap collection, and most of what a building is specified in. |
| SW 9001 to SW 9186 | Colors added to the collection later. |
| Some between SW 1015 and SW 2865, and SW 8917 | Older and historic colors still on the list. |

## Reading a Code

- **`SW` and four digits, then a name:** `SW 7069 Iron Ore`. The number is what counts; names repeat across paint brands.
- **The locator number,** such as `251-C7`, says where the chip sits on the ColorSnap display in a store. Use it to find the chip, never to order paint.
- **Red, green and blue,** and the **hex code** made from them, are Sherwin-Williams' own screen values for the color, the kind of value the [Color Matcher](/colors/) compares.

## How We Use It

- **Matching a building.** A landlord's criteria or an architect's elevations give the wall or fascia as an SW number. A raceway, a backer or a cabinet that should disappear is painted to match it.
- **Finding the paint.** Look the SW number up in the [Color Library](/colors/library/): the office's Matthews formulas are listed by the color they match, Sherwin-Williams among them.
- **Finding the nearest.** With only a hex code or a photo's color, the [Color Matcher](/colors/) returns the closest Sherwin-Williams colors, each with a percent match.
- **On a drawing,** give the SW number and name, then the Matthews formula that stands in for it, as in *PAINTED TO MATCH SW 7069 IRON ORE*.

## Finishes and Notes

- The chart gives colors only. Sheen (flat, satin, semi-gloss, gloss) belongs to the paint product, and on a sign it's the Matthews gloss level that's called out.
- The same color in house paint and in sign paint won't look identical side by side: the gloss, and the surface under it, change how it reads. Ask for a sprayed sample when the match matters.
- The list is from 2019, in a copy saved in 2021. Sherwin-Williams adds and retires colors every year, so check a number that isn't here on its site.
- Every swatch below is drawn from the chart's own hex code. Pick from a real chip, never from this page.
