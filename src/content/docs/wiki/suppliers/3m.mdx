## Product Lines

Two families: translucent films, which light passes through, and opaque films, which block it.

| Series | Family | What It Is |
|---|---|---|
| Scotchcal Translucent 3630 | Translucent | The standard film for lit faces. Vinyl, 2 mil thick, with a clear permanent adhesive. |
| Envision Translucent 3730 | Translucent | Made for faces lit by LEDs. Its color numbers end in `L`. |
| Scotchcal ElectroCut 7725 | Opaque | Cut graphics with a permanent adhesive. Cuts and weeds down to 1/4" letters. |
| Scotchcal ElectroCut 7125 | Opaque | Like 7725. Cuts and weeds down to 3/8" letters. |
| Controltac with Comply 180mC | Opaque | Slides into place before it's pressed down, and lets trapped air out. For large graphics and wraps. |
| Scotchcal Series 50 | Opaque | A separate, shorter color range with its own numbers. |
| Fluorescent 7725SE | Opaque | Five fluorescent colors, in this series only. |

## Reading a Code

- **Series, a dash, then the color number:** `3630-33` is Scotchcal Translucent 3630 in Red.
- **A color keeps its number across the series that share a chart.** Black is 12 in 7725, 7125 and 180mC alike: `7725-12`, `180mC-12`. The translucent pair share numbers too: `3630-33` and `3730-33L` are both Red. They aren't the same color, though: the chart gives the two a different Pantone match.
- **`L` at the end** of a 3730 number means the color is tuned for LED light.
- **Series 50 has its own numbers.** Its Burgundy is 49; the other opaque films' Burgundy is 58.
- **White is 10** in 7725, 7125 and 180mC, and each has its own white film, as the chart notes.

## How We Use It

- **On a detail,** a blank that reads *WITH ______ 3M VINYL* takes a translucent film: the full number and the name, as in *3/16" 7328 WHITE ACRYLIC WITH 3630-33 RED 3M VINYL*. The [Typical Signage Types](/wiki/signage-types/) show where those blanks are.
- **Lit faces take translucent film, always.** Opaque film on a lit face shows as a dark shape. That is the point when a letter should read black at night, and a mistake otherwise.
- **Push-thru letters** are backed with white translucent film (White is `3630-20`), with the color on the face.
- **For a brand color,** read the Pantone column below for the film 3M matched to it, or give the [Color Matcher](/colors/) a hex code: it returns the nearest 3630 colors with a percent match.
- **Paint and film rarely match exactly.** Where a film sits beside a [Matthews](/wiki/suppliers/matthews-paint/) color, compare real samples.

## Finishes and Notes

- **A translucent film has two colors:** one lit from behind at night, one by daylight. The charts show it unlit. Check a film lit before promising a color.
- **3630 and 3730 go on** acrylic, polycarbonate and flexible sign faces, wet or dry. Applied by hand, wet is safer: dry leaves streaks and bubbles that show when lit.
- **3730 lets more light through:** 3M's chart says most graphics in 3730 on its diffuser film 3735-50 pass more light than 3630 on diffuser film 3635-30.
- **The opaque chart names its finishes** where a color has one: Matte White, Matte Black, Satin Aluminum, Satin Gold and the metallics.
- The translucent chart is 3M's European one from 2017, and the opaque chart its United States one from 2016. Colors come and go by region and year, so confirm a film with the vinyl supplier before it goes on a drawing.
- A Pantone number beside a film is the closest standard 3M names, a simulation and nothing more.
- Every swatch below is the chart's color as a screen shows it. Pick from a 3M swatch book, never from this page.
