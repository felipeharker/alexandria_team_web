## How to Use It

| Step | What |
|---|---|
| 1 · Translate | [Braille Lettering Designer](/braille/) · Grade 2 · copy the braille characters |
| 2 · Paste | CorelDRAW, Illustrator, Word · font Alexandria Braille |
| 3 · Size | 36 pt · lines 28.8 pt apart |
| 4 · Curves | Convert to curves · one circle per dot |

## Its Sizes at 36 pt

| | Inches | ADA 703.3.1 |
|---|---|---|
| Dot diameter | 0.06 | 0.059 to 0.063 |
| Dots apart in a cell | 0.1 | 0.09 to 0.1 |
| Cells apart | 0.25 | 0.241 to 0.3 |
| Lines apart | 0.4 | 0.395 to 0.4 |

Centre to centre · the Braille Lettering Designer's sizes

## What It Has

| Typed or Pasted | Gives |
|---|---|
| Braille characters, `⠗⠑⠌` | Themselves · all 256, six and eight dots |
| Space | A blank cell |
| Letters, a to z | Each letter's cell, uncontracted · mock-ups only |
| Numbers, punctuation | Nothing · translate them |

Contractions, capitals and number signs are translation · a font can't add them
