## Design Tools

Web tools for the design package's graphics: the site plan, a client's logo as vectors, and patterned panels.

### Site Plan

Finds an address and draws the office's greyscale site plan around it: buildings, streets, parks and water from OpenStreetMap, in one standard style. Place the sign with a click, size a dashed red circle around it with *SIGN LOCATION* inside, and export the map as a PNG for the design package.

### Vectorizer

Turns a PNG or JPG, such as a client's logo, into an SVG of clean vector shapes, one layer per color. Keep the colors you want, leave out the background, set the precision, and download the SVG for Rhino, CorelDRAW or fabrication. The image never leaves your browser.

### Parametric Grid

Lays out a square, hex or triangle grid on a panel and scales each cell toward a point or a curve you place on the preview, from a Min size at the attractor to a Max size away from it. Download the linework as an SVG at full size, one shape per cell, for Rhino, CorelDRAW or fabrication.

## Fabrication Tools

Web tools for building it: matching colors to paint and vinyl, the office's paint formulas, and pricing the materials.

### Color Matcher

Turns any hex code into its nearest Sherwin-Williams, Pantone (Solid Coated) and 3M Scotchcal 3630 colors, each with a percent match based on how alike the two colors look (CIEDE2000). Where the office already has a Matthews Paint formula for the matched color, it says so. Lists import and export as CSV.

### Material Estimator

<span class="ax-badge">Beta</span> Still being built, so some of it may change or not work yet.

Prices a project's materials. Add a line per material, pick how it's priced (linear feet, square feet, cubic yards and the rest), and enter the unit price and the quantity: the sheet totals it all by line, by pricing unit and by category. Sheets save in your browser and move between people as CSV files, which open in Excel too.
