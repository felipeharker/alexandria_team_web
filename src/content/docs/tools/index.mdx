Web tools for the office's everyday work. They run in your browser, with nothing to install.

## Material Estimator

Prices a project's materials. Add a line per material, pick how it's priced (linear feet, square feet, cubic yards and the rest), and enter the unit price and the quantity: the sheet totals it all by line, by pricing unit and by category. Sheets save in your browser and move between people as CSV files, which open in Excel too.

## Color Matcher

Turns any hex code into its nearest Sherwin-Williams, Pantone (Solid Coated) and 3M Scotchcal 3630 colors, each with a percent match based on how alike the two colors look (CIEDE2000). Where the office already has a Matthews Paint formula for the matched color, it says so. Lists import and export as CSV.
