Web tools for the design package's graphics. They run in your browser, with nothing to install.

## Site Plan

Finds an address and draws the office's greyscale site plan around it: buildings, streets, parks and water from OpenStreetMap, in one standard style. Place the sign with a click, size a dashed circle around it with *SIGN LOCATION* inside (red, or a color of your own per sign), and export the map as a PNG for the design package, with a north arrow and scale bar.

## Vectorizer

Turns a PNG or JPG, such as a client's logo, into an SVG of clean vector shapes, one layer per color. Keep the colors you want, leave out the background, set the precision, and download the SVG for Rhino, CorelDRAW or fabrication. The image never leaves your browser.

## Parametric Grid

Lays out a square, hex or triangle grid on a panel and scales each cell toward a point or a curve you place on the preview, from a Min size at the attractor to a Max size away from it. Download the linework as an SVG at full size, one shape per cell, for Rhino, CorelDRAW or fabrication.

## Braille Lettering Designer

Type a sign's text and download its braille as an SVG: one circle per raised dot, in inches, at the ADA's sizes, to place below the raised lettering in Rhino, CorelDRAW or Illustrator. It writes uncontracted (Grade 1) braille; ADA signs use contracted (Grade 2), which can be pasted in as braille characters from a braille translator.
