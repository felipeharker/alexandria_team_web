Web tools for patterned panels: a grid of cells, each sized by a rule, saved as full-size linework for Rhino, CorelDRAW or fabrication. They run in your browser, with nothing to install, and lay out the same square, hex and triangle grids.

## Parametric Grid

Lays out a square, hex or triangle grid on a panel and scales each cell toward points and curves you place on the preview, from a Min size at the attractor to a Max size away from it. Download the linework as an SVG at full size, one shape per cell.

## Image Grid

<span class="ax-badge">Beta</span> New, so some of it may change.

The same grids, with each cell sized by the image under it instead, as Grasshopper's Image Sampler does: large where the image is dark and small where it's light, or the other way round. Open a photo, a logo or a gradient, and the panel's cells show it, as a halftone does. **Simple** shows the essentials; **Standard** adds the image's colors or opacity, tone, a set number of sizes for the punches or drills you have, circles in place of the cells, and a random cull. The image never leaves your browser.
