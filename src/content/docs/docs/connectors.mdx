|---|---|---|
| SquareCoil | Projects, design requests and customers, as your own login sees them. Read-only | *What's due this week?* |

| CorelDRAW | The open CorelDRAW document | *List the fonts on this page.* |

## On Your PC

<Steps>

1. **Alexandria Setup** on Rhino's Alexandria Tools tab installs what all three run on your PC. **Install Connectors**, beside it, installs or updates one at a time. No Rhino on the PC: [AI connectors](/wiki/downloads/#ai-connectors) is the same installer.

2. Type your own SquareCoil login when it asks, once.

3. Set up before 2.22? Each connector may show twice in Claude. Run **Install Connectors** once, then quit Claude Desktop from its tray icon and open it again.

</Steps>
