## On Your PC

<Steps>

1. **Alexandria Setup** on Rhino's Alexandria Tools tab installs all three. **Install Connectors**, beside it, installs or updates one at a time. No Rhino on the PC: [Claude connectors](/wiki/downloads/#claude-connectors) is the same installer.

2. Type your own SquareCoil login when it asks, once.

3. Quit Claude Desktop from its tray icon and open it again.

</Steps>

## Claude

Ready. The three are on for every team account, in Claude Desktop and Claude Code.
