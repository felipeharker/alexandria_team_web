1. **Install both packages** · [Install and Update](/docs/install/) · about ten minutes · no office drive: the [zip](/start/download/)

2. **`AlexandriaSetup`** in Rhino · Enter

3. **Practice** · **File › New › Alexandria** · **Create Project** in a scratch folder · every button: the [Rhino guide](/rhino/) · a finished package: the [Sample Project](/sample-project/)

## Good to Know

- **Export your work** · saves in that browser only · name the project, then export into its folder
- **Updates are automatic** · web tools: every visit · Rhino tools: at startup, then **Alexandria Setup** once more
- **Stuck** · [Troubleshooting](/start/troubleshooting/) · or the Alexandria team
